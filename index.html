About this demo

  • Sample data only: every page shows fictional sample data. You can click around and edit, but nothing is saved, and reloading resets it.
  • Full versions save to your own browser and add Export, Import and Clear. Nothing is ever sent to a server.
  • No tracking: no analytics, no accounts, no third-party requests.

Sources

Facts in these tools come from Grad-IQ's Sep 30 and Oct 28, 2026 research scans of Reddit (10 education subreddits) and the open web (Academia Stack Exchange, surveys, official sources). Each tool cites its sources inline. Key sources:

  • IRS Publication 970: taxability of stipends and fellowships
  • ETS summary of the 2026 federal student-loan changes: loan caps, Grad PLUS, RAP, SAVE transition
  • Inside Higher Ed Student Voice, Oct 2025: surprise expenses and emergency aid
  • Nature Graduate Survey 2025: PhD finances and mental health
  • Dunlosky et al. 2013: practice testing and spaced practice
  • SIU / Herff Jones fall 2026 doctoral regalia order form: new regalia prices
  • NACE 2026 Student Survey and ZipRecruiter Graduate Survey 2026: job offers and work experience (Launch Pad)
  • Barclays Scams Bulletin, Sep 2026 and FTC: Job scams: job-scam checks
  • Bank of America Better Money Habits, May 2026: living paycheck to paycheck, cutting back on dining out
  • USDA: SNAP and students and HHS: LIHEAP: help finder
  • Reddit and Academia Stack Exchange threads, linked where they're used
