30-day reset planner

Stop trying to fix everything at once. Pick one area a week, start with just 10 minutes, and keep proof that you're changing. Missing a day is normal; the rule here is never miss twice.

Inspired by r/getdisciplined's most-upvoted posts this month, including "Pick one area per week" and "Procrastination isn't a laziness problem, it's a mood problem".

One focus area per week

Five areas: sleep, phone, body, money and learning. Thirty days gives you four focus weeks plus a 2-day wrap-up, so save one area for your next reset.

10-minute start

Can't start? Notice how you feel, then do just 10 minutes. When the timer ends you can stop, and it still counts.

Habit grid: never miss twice

Tap a day to mark it done. A missed day shows ✕. Two misses in a row turn red, so the day after a miss is the one to protect, even with a tiny version.

Screen-time log

Copy the screen-time number from your phone's settings each day, pick a replacement for the scroll urge, and tick the box if the phone stayed out of bed.

Weekly proof journal

Evidence beats motivation. Write down what you actually did, however small.
