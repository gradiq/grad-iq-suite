Budget & money · Tool 16

Paycheck-to-paycheck planner

Not a monthly budget. This planner works one paycheck at a time: which bills each paycheck covers, and one number for what's safe to spend this week. 42% of Gen Z say they live paycheck to paycheck, so you're far from alone.

Source: Bank of America Better Money Habits, May 19, 2026 (Ipsos survey of 1,133 Gen Z adults). For a category-by-category monthly plan, see the Stipend budget planner.

Safe to spend

Log spending

Paycheck calendar

Add each paycheck you expect, then give every bill to the paycheck that pays it. "Auto-assign" picks the last paycheck on or before each due date.

Bills

Subscription audit

Look at your bank and card statements and list every recurring charge. Decide: keep, pause or cancel.

Sinking funds

Irregular costs you know are coming (car repairs, holidays, textbooks). Each fund is split across the paychecks you've listed before its date and set aside from them.

Help finder

Asking for help is normal and smart. Links go to official government sites and open in a new tab. Rules change, so check the linked page or your campus office.
