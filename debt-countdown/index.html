Debt-free countdown

For credit cards and other non-student debt. Enter what you owe, and see when you could be debt-free paying smallest-balance-first (snowball) or highest-rate-first (avalanche). Student loans? Use the Loan repayment plan tracker.

All payoff dates and interest totals are estimates. They assume your APRs and minimums stay the same, no new charges, and interest added once a month at APR ÷ 12. Card issuers usually charge interest on your average daily balance, so your real numbers will differ a little. Check your statements.

Call script: ask for a lower APR

  • Call the number on the back of the card. If the first answer is no, politely ask if someone else can review it.
  • Ask whether a new rate is permanent or promotional, and when it starts.
  • Ask whether the review involves a credit check.
  • Write down the date, the person's name and the answer below.
