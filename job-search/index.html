Is this job real? Scam check

In a UK survey of Gen Z jobseekers, 69% had applied for a role without checking it was genuine, and 45% believe they've been targeted by a job scam. Each card on the board has its own scam check. The rules of thumb:

  • Find the job on the employer's own website, and contact them using details you found yourself.
  • Honest employers never ask you to pay for a job, training or equipment.
  • Never deposit a check and send part of it back, buy gift cards, or move money through your account for someone.
  • Be careful with ID, Social Security or bank details before a real interview.
  • Ignore unexpected job texts and WhatsApp or Telegram messages.

Sources: Barclays Scams Bulletin, Sep 10, 2026 (UK adults, Opinium, Aug 2026); FTC: Job scams. Report scams at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

No-experience experience builder

Experience counts even when it isn't a "real job". In ZipRecruiter's 2026 survey, 81.6% of recent grads who worked during college were employed, compared with 40.7% of those who didn't. List campus jobs, projects, volunteering and clubs, and turn each into a resume bullet.

Source: ZipRecruiter Graduate Survey 2026 (1,500 recent grads, Jan–Mar 2026).
